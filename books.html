Divya

Techno-thriller · coming soon

On the night of her marriage, Divya leaves her home in her husband's car.

She takes a gun nobody knew she owned. At the gate she stops for the barrier, calm, and drives out into the dark at three in the morning.

Her road runs into two other men. A Chennai policeman who cannot close a file, and who keeps working a case his own station closed a week ago. And an old swami on the bank of the Ganga, loved by millions, who teaches that everything a person holds passes, and that none of it ever belonged to them.

Whatever went wrong in that flat, no law covers it. It runs like a system, and it ran long before Divya drew breath.

Partners in Crime

Psychological thriller

Arun has everything figured out. A thriving tech company, a family he adores, a life built on careful decisions and controlled outcomes. When he books a vacation to the Andaman Islands, he expects nothing more than turquoise water, lazy mornings, and uninterrupted time with his wife and daughters.

But the island has other plans.

Trapped with nowhere to run, Arun finds that some debts don't dissolve with time. Some reckonings arrive quietly, patiently, wearing familiar faces and carrying the weight of everything left unsaid.
