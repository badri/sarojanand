### 1. ✅ "The Temple That Shouldn't Exist: Kailasa, Sigiriya, and the Case for a Lost Engineering Canon"
**Why this wins:**
- Visual, mind-bending
- Indian pride hook
- Sujatha-style engineering curiosity
- Blends history + science + speculation
- Strong first impression

**Seeds:** Kailasa Temple, Sigiriya, ancient civ destroyed in 0.00002s, Silurian Hypothesis
