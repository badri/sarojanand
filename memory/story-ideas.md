
## Infrastructure Corruption Thriller (Feb 8, 2026)

**Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VEu8biALJ8s (Pothole documentary)

**Premise:** Whistleblower engineer discovers road construction cartel, attempts to expose it, gets silenced.

**Real cases to draw from:**
- Satyendra Dubey (2003) — NHI engineer, reported Golden Quadrilateral corruption, identity leaked by PMO to accused, murdered as "robbery gone wrong." Led to Whistleblowers Protection Act 2014.
- Manoj Kumar Gupta (2008) — UP engineer, refused extortion, kidnapped/tortured/killed by MLA. MLA got life sentence.
- 46,300 crore BBMP complaint (2024) — 4,100 pages of evidence, 75% siphoned, still ongoing.

**Story beats:**
1. Honest contractor wins tender (like Ricondo brothers)
2. Discovers why previous contractors failed — not incompetence, deliberate sabotage
3. Refuses to play along
4. Threatens to expose
5. System closes in — bureaucrats, politicians, mafia
6. Race to get evidence out before silenced

**Sujatha angle:** Technical details of road construction (subgrade, subbase, bitumen grades), forensic investigation of why roads fail, the math of corruption (105 cr honest vs 340 cr corrupt over 20 years).

**Title ideas:** "The Pin Code" (roads as ATM, potholes as PIN), "Subgrade"

**Tone:** Investigative thriller, Sujatha-style technical curiosity meets systemic rot
